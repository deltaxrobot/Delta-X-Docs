Delta X S — Control guide¶

Use the Termite guide for X3 and XS to connect over USB, configure the serial port, home the robot, and send your first movement commands.

Select your robot reference¶

  • Delta X S V4 G-code reference
  • Delta X S V5 G-code reference

Choose the reference for your robot version and use coordinates within your model’s workspace. The Termite guide explains absolute positioning with G90; select a starting Z coordinate appropriate to your XS size before trying the movement example.
